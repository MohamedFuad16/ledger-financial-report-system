# Bakuraku customer research

_Research checked: 2026-08-20 (Asia/Tokyo)_

## Outcome

- **112 distinct confirmed customer organizations** were identified from **120 first-party customer stories** in the official [Bakuraku case-study archive](https://bakuraku.jp/case/).
- **0 prospect companies were added.** The confirmed set already exceeds the requested roughly 100-company research target.
- Six organizations have two stories each; JOCA appears under two typography variants; STORES appears once under its former name, hey. These were merged rather than double-counted.
- Every customer classification is backed by a direct Bakuraku case-study URL. The official site supplies company name, industry, and product tags; repeat stories were unioned.
- Official company homepages were located for 111 of 112 organizations. No separate official homepage was found for 若原義敬税理士事務所, so that cell is intentionally blank rather than guessed.

## Interpretation and limitations

- This is a **publicly evidenced customer-story corpus**, not LayerX's private customer database. Absence from the table does not imply that an organization is not a customer.
- A published story proves use at publication time; it does not guarantee the contract remains active on the research date.
- “All” means all distinct companies exposed by the ten-page public case archive when checked. Search/archive pagination and later editorial changes can alter the count.
- Product use is limited to products tagged on the official case pages. It may understate products adopted later or products discussed only in prose.
- Official company website URLs are provided for enrichment, but the **evidence URL**, not the company homepage, is the source for the Bakuraku-customer claim.
